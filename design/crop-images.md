# Crop-specific produce images

Image selection uses the listing's crop name, never its broad category. Unknown, mixed and unsupported variants show a neutral `Photo not supplied` placeholder. The resolver is shared by home, catalogue, listing details, market comparisons and cart.

New representative images were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool and inspected individually. They are not photos of sellers' lots. Generated images are labelled in the UI. Existing tomato, carrot and red pepper assets remain reused only for matching crop names.

Saved runtime assets: `assets/potatoes-still-life.webp`, `assets/produce/{onion,brinjal,okra,chilli,cabbage,cauliflower,cucumber,spinach}.webp`. Neutral vector: `assets/produce-photo-placeholder.svg`.
