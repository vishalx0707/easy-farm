## Editorial notes

The product shots are recordings of the current EasyFarm interface with an isolated sample grower, buyer and tomato order, composited into the film. The market price screen shows asking prices from active listings. The [audio cues](easyfarm-audio-cues.md) mark the transition and action sounds.
